# sting / stinct — 刺、分离、熄灭

## 核心含义

拉丁语 _stinguere_ = to prick, to mark, to quench。这个词根看似有三条不相关的含义，但都源于同一个动作——「刺」：

1. **"刺出标记 → 区分"** → distinguish, distinct, distinction
2. **"刺灭（火焰）→ 熄灭/灭绝"** → extinguish, extinct, extinction
3. **"从内部刺痛 → 内在冲动"** → instinct

## 派生词网络

### Tier 1 — 雅思高频核心

| 单词 | 拆解 | 字面义 | 实际义 |
|------|------|--------|--------|
| **distinguish** | dis-(分开) + stinguish(刺→标记) | 刺出标记来区分 | 区分、辨别 |
| **distinct** | dis-(分开) + stinct(刺→标记) | 区分开来的 | 明显不同的、清晰的 |
| **extinct** | ex-(出) + stinct(熄灭) | 彻底熄灭的 | 灭绝的、绝迹的 |

### Tier 2 — 重要扩展

| 单词 | 拆解 | 字面义 | 实际义 |
|------|------|--------|--------|
| **distinction** | distinct + -ion | 区分的行为 | 区别、差别、优秀 |
| **distinctive** | distinct + -ive | 能区分的 | 独特的、有特色的 |
| **extinguish** | ex-(出) + stinguish(熄灭) | 彻底熄灭 | 熄灭（火）、消灭 |
| **extinction** | extinct + -ion | 灭绝的状态 | 灭绝、消亡 |
| **instinct** | in-(内) + stinct(刺) | 内在的刺痛 | 本能、直觉 |

## 例句（IELTS 场景）

- **distinguish** — *It is crucial to distinguish between correlation and causation in research.*
  - 写作 Task 2 论证万能句：distinguish A from B / between A and B
- **distinct** — *The two concepts are related but distinct — one refers to cause, the other to effect.*
  - 写作限定概念：distinct from / a distinct advantage
- **extinct** — *Numerous species have become extinct due to human activities.*
  - 写作 Task 2 环境话题：become extinct / an extinct species
- **distinction** — *A clear distinction must be made between legal and moral obligations.*
  - 写作定义概念：make a distinction / without distinction
- **distinctive** — *Each culture has its own distinctive traditions and values.*
  - 写作/口语描述特征：a distinctive feature / style
- **extinction** — *The extinction of bees would have catastrophic consequences for agriculture.*
  - 写作 Task 2 环境话题
- **instinct** — *Our survival instinct drives us to avoid danger and seek safety.*
  - 写作/口语讨论行为：by instinct / natural instinct

## 前缀拆解公式

```
[前缀] + sting/stinct/stinguish

dis- 分开 + stinguish → distinguish  区分（刺出标记分开来）
dis- 分开 + stinct    → distinct     明显的（分开标记的）
ex-  出   + stinguish → extinguish  熄灭（彻底刺灭）
ex-  出   + stinct    → extinct     灭绝（彻底熄灭的）
in-  内   + stinct    → instinct    本能（内在的刺痛→冲动）
```

## 易混辨析

- **distinguish** vs **differentiate**：写作可互换，distinguish 更常用（distinguish between / from），differentiate 更正式
- **distinct** vs **distinctive**：distinct = 不同的、清晰的（a distinct accent），distinctive = 独特的、有辨识度的（a distinctive voice）
- **extinct** vs **endangered**：extinct = 已灭绝，endangered = 濒危（还没灭绝）——写作环境话题必须分清
- **instinct** vs **intuition**：instinct = 本能（生物性的），intuition = 直觉（经验性的）
