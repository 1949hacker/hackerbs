# tend / tens / tent — 伸展、朝向

## 核心含义

拉丁语 *tendere* = to stretch, to extend, to aim toward。派生词围绕"伸展"展开四条线：

1. **"向某方向伸展 → 意图/趋势"** → intend, tendency, tend
2. **"向外伸展 → 扩大/范围"** → extend, extent, extensive
3. **"伸展到紧张 → 张力/强烈"** → tension, tense, intense, intensive
4. **"伸向某人 → 关注/照顾"** → attend, attention, attentive

## 派生词网络

### Tier 1 — 雅思高频核心（必须掌握）

| 单词 | 拆解 | 字面义 | 实际义 |
|------|------|--------|--------|
| **tend** | tend(伸展→朝向) | 伸向某个方向 | 倾向于、往往会 |
| **intend** | in-(向) + tend(伸展) | 向某方向伸展 | 打算、计划 |
| **intention** | intend + -tion | 伸展的方向 | 意图、目的 |
| **extend** | ex-(出) + tend(伸展) | 伸出去 | 扩展、延长、提供 |
| **extent** | ex-(出) + tent(伸展) | 伸展到的范围 | 程度、范围 |
| **attend** | at-(=ad,向) + tend(伸展) | 伸向某人 | 出席、照料 |
| **attention** | attend + -tion | 伸向某人的状态 | 注意力、关注 |
| **tension** | tens(伸展) + -ion | 被拉紧的状态 | 紧张、张力 |
| **intense** | in-(内) + tense(伸展) | 内心拉紧 | 强烈的、激烈的 |
| **intensive** | intense + -ive | 高强度的 | 加强的、密集的 |

## 例句（IELTS 场景）

- **tend** — *People tend to spend more money during holiday seasons.*
  - 写作描述趋势：tend to = 往往会
- **intend** — *The government intends to introduce new environmental regulations.*
  - 写作 Task 2：intend to do / be intended to + verb
- **intention** — *The intention of the policy is to reduce income inequality.*
  - 写作：with the intention of
- **extend** — *The deadline has been extended by two weeks.*
  - 写作/听力：extend an offer / deadline / visa
- **extent** — *To a large extent, the success of a business depends on customer satisfaction.*
  - 写作 Task 2 万能：to some / a large / a certain extent
- **attend** — *Students are required to attend at least 80% of classes.*
  - 写作/口语：attend school / a meeting / a conference
- **attention** — *Particular attention should be paid to early childhood education.*
  - 写作：pay attention to / draw attention to / bring to one's attention
- **tension** — *Racial tensions have increased in many multicultural societies.*
  - 写作 Task 2 社会话题：social / racial / political tension
- **intense** — *The competition for university places is extremely intense.*
  - 写作描述竞争/压力
- **intensive** — *The patient required intensive care after the surgery.*
  - 写作/阅读：intensive course / farming / training
- **extensive** — *Extensive research has been conducted on the effects of climate change.*
  - 写作 Task 2：extensive research / experience / damage
- **tendency** — *There is a growing tendency for young people to delay marriage.*
  - 写作描述趋势：a tendency to do / toward
- **intentionally** — *The company intentionally misled consumers about the product's safety.*
  - 写作讨论企业道德
- **pretend** — *We cannot pretend that climate change does not exist.*
  - 口语/写作

## 前缀拆解公式

```
[前缀] + tend/tens/tent

in-  向   + tend → intend      打算（朝某方向）
ex-  出   + tend → extend      扩展（伸出去）
at-  向   + tend → attend      出席（伸向某人）
pre- 前   + tend → pretend     假装（伸在前面掩盖）
con- 一起 + tend → contend     竞争（一起拉/主张）
in-  内   + tense → intense   强烈（内心拉紧）
```

## 易混辨析

- **extend** vs **expand**：extend = 线性延伸（时间/长度），expand = 空间扩张（体积/规模）
- **extent** vs **degree**：extent 偏"覆盖范围"，degree 偏"等级/程度"
- **intense** vs **intensive**：intense = 强度大（intense pain），intensive = 专注/密集（intensive course）
- **attend** vs **present**：attend 强调行为（出席），present 强调状态（在场）
- **intentional** vs **deliberate**：几乎同义，deliberate 更强调"深思熟虑的"
